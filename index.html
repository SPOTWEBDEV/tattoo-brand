About the studio

A private, appointment-only studio specializing in fine line and custom blackwork, based on the Lower East Side of Manhattan. Every piece starts as a one-on-one consultation so the design fits the body and the client, not a flash sheet.

Frequently asked questions

Is the $50 deposit refundable?

The deposit confirms your slot and comes off the final price. It's non-refundable within 48 hours of the appointment, but freely reschedulable with more notice than that.

How does the home service travel fee work?

In-New-York home service has no added fee. Elsewhere in the U.S. adds a flat $200. International bookings add a flat $1,200. It's calculated automatically once you enter your address.

Can I customize one of the gallery designs?

Yes — gallery pieces are a starting point. Mention your changes in the special instructions field at checkout and they'll be worked into the pre-session consultation.

Do you tattoo minors?

No — clients must be 18 or older with valid government ID, no exceptions, matching New York State law.

What if I need a touch-up later?

Free touch-ups are included within 60 days of the original session for studio appointments; home service touch-ups are subject to the same travel fee structure.
